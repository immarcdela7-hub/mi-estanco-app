Common Questions

Everything you need to know, whether you are visiting or hosting.

For Travelers

Do I have to spend my refund in Barcelona?

Not at all! You are in total control. You can use your pending VAT to unlock local discounts, or simply select "Bank Transfer" to receive your money in your home currency when you leave.

For Merchants

Do I need new hardware to accept NTL?

No. The tourist pays the full price with their standard Visa/Mastercard. The NTL app detects the transaction via Open Banking and processes the discount on our end. Zero friction for your staff.

For Travelers

How fast do I get paid?

The moment you scan your digital DIVA code at the airport customs machine and it turns green, our system clears your funds instantly. No 60-day waits.

Security & Legal

Is this officially recognized?

Yes. NTL is fully integrated with the Spanish Tax Agency (AEAT) DIVA system and complies with all EU VAT refund directives (2006/112/CE).
